# Articles

Writing about the engineering behind this product: what was built, what it cost, and what was decided against.

Every claim in a piece here resolves to something a reader can check — code in the
[repository](https://github.com/akarazhev/bitcoin-risk-brief), a live endpoint, or a page on this site. Nothing
asserts adoption, accuracy, or investment value, and nothing frames the product as financial advice.

## Published

That gap is deliberate rather than an oversight. A draft is raw material — structure, verified facts, working links —
and a piece that reads as machine-written costs more than no piece at all. `articles/` in the repository holds drafts;
this section holds only what has been through the author's own voice.

## Where a piece appears

The version here is canonical. Syndicated copies elsewhere point back to it:

| Venue | Relationship |
| --- | --- |
| This site | The original. Every syndicated copy sets its canonical URL to the page here. |
| dev.to | English syndication, with `canonical_url` set. |
| Habr | Russian syndication, marked as a translation of the original. |

A syndicated copy that does not declare its canonical splits the same text across two addresses and teaches search
engines that neither is authoritative, which is worth more care than it usually gets.
