# Bitcoin Risk Brief Documentation

Bitcoin Risk Brief is a production-pilot analytics product that computes a focused daily Bitcoin risk signal from the
canonical BTC/USD CSV and exposes its freshness and readiness state. It provides research context only—not financial
advice, investment advice, or a trading recommendation.
